## Problem
#31 (PR #157) added `appendHistory()`, a `runTransaction` on the live `state/history`. Two write paths merged the same day still replace the whole array from the local cache:
- `adjustBonus()` (`src/bonus.js:40`) appends with `patchRoot({ history: state.history.concat(entry).slice(-500) })`, and its undo (`:46`) writes a filtered copy of the whole array.
- Uncheck (`undoCompletionRecords`, `src/tasks.js:210`) removes one entry by writing `patchRoot({ history: rest })`.

If a kid ticks a chore on the TV-side tablet while a parent gives a bonus or unchecks on a phone, one of the two history entries is lost. Each write also re-sends up to 500 entries, and every device downloads them again. Separately, bonus entries have no `taskName`, and the draft `firebase-rules.json` requires `history/$i` to have `taskName` and `person`. Once JT applies those rules, every bonus history write will be rejected after the points have already moved.
