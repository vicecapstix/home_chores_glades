## Problem
Found by the weekly deep bug hunt (reproduced with a failing Vitest test). When ticking a chore, `applyToggle` (`src/tasks.js` ~364-371) writes the task first via `patchTask`, which `trackWrite` (`src/pending.js` 26-44) records for "⚠ not saved — tap to retry". If that write fails, `applyToggle` returns before awarding points, streak, badges or history. Tapping retry (`retrySync`) replays **only** the task update (`done:true, lastDoneBy, lastDonePoints, …`). Result: the chore is done and says Al earned 1 pt, but Al got 0 points and no history line. A later uncheck then takes back points Al never received (an admin uncheck can push the balance below 0).
Mirror case on uncheck: points are reversed, then put back when `patchTask` fails; retry then unchecks the chore while the member keeps the points.

## Outcome / acceptance criteria
- [ ] Retrying a failed check/uncheck gives the same end state as a successful first tap: task fields, member points/weekPoints/count/streak/badges and history all agree
- [ ] Or (simpler, acceptable): a failed toggle is not offered as a raw task-write retry; "tap to retry" re-runs the whole toggle (`toggleTask`) against current state, or the tag says "not saved — tap the chore again"
- [ ] Retrying twice cannot award twice (the in-flight guard from #218 still applies)
- [ ] Non-toggle task writes (edit, subtasks) keep today's retry behaviour

## Affected users
Members and admins ticking chores on a flaky connection (phones/tablets); leaderboard and TV show wrong totals.
