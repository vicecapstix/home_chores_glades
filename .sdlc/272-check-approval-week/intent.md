# Intent — #272 A parent check approved after the week rolls over counts in both weeks


## Problem
Found by the weekly deep bug hunt (reproduced). For a "Needs parent check" chore, `approveCheck` calls `applyToggle(id, t, p.by, p.at, …)` so `nowTs` is the child's tick time. History uses `completedAt: nowTs` (child's tick), but `lastDoneWeek` and the `weekStart` passed to `adjustMemberPoints` use `getWeekStart()` = the approval time (`src/tasks.js` ~340 and ~349).
Scenario: Bo ticks a medium weekly chore Sun 1 Nov 20:00; a parent taps "Looks good" Mon 2 Nov 08:00. Bo's `weekPoints` for the new week becomes 6 (This week board, TV strip), and history puts the same 6 in last week, so he is also last week's champion. Team goal (from history) and `weekPoints` disagree.

## Outcome / acceptance criteria
- [ ] Points from an approved check are counted in exactly one week, the week of the child's tick (`p.at`), consistently for `weekPoints`, `lastDoneWeek` and history
- [ ] If the tick's week is no longer the member's current week, `weekPoints` is not increased (all-time `points` still is)
- [ ] Unchecking that chore later reverses the right week (`lastDoneWeek`)
- [ ] Approval in the same week behaves exactly as today

## Affected users
Members whose chores need a parent check; admin approving on Monday morning; leaderboard and TV.

No Blaze-only features (Functions, Storage, Extensions), no new services, API keys, secrets or paid hosting. Keep data bounded (history cap 500, redemptions cap 100) and reads small. Never write to or change rules on the live DB; tests mock Firebase.

## Open questions
None — counting by the child's tick time matches history, the double-points rule (#225) and the streak date.
