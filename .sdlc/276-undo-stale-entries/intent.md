# Intent — #276 After one Undo, older undo entries stay live

Queue position: 10

## Problem
Found by the weekly deep bug hunt (reproduced). `popUndo` (`src/undo.js` ~15-17) hides the toast and clears its timer but leaves older entries on `_stack`. Admin deletes chore A, then chore B 2 s later, then clicks Undo (B restored). The toast fades (opacity 0), but `#undo-btn` keeps keyboard focus and `.toast` only sets `pointer-events:none`. An hour later, pressing Enter/Space restores chore A, long after its 5 s window. CLAUDE.md says expiry clears all pending entries.

## Outcome / acceptance criteria
- [ ] After an Undo, or when the 5 s window ends, no older entry can be undone (stack cleared, or each entry carries its own expiry)
- [ ] A hidden undo toast's button is not focusable/activatable (e.g. `hidden`/`inert` or blur on hide), keeping the #163 accessibility fix
- [ ] Two quick deletes + Undo inside the window still restores the latest one

Admins (keyboard users, TV remotes, tablets with keyboards).
